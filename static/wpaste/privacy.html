WPaste Privacy Policy

简体中文

The Mac App Store version of WPaste saves clipboard history only on your Mac so you can find and copy items again. History may include text, links, images, file paths, the source app and the time of copying. File items store a reference to the file. Treat your history as sensitive.

You can pause recording from the menu bar, exclude apps or content patterns, set retention limits and delete individual items or clear your history in Settings. WPaste excludes clipboard items marked as concealed. A password copied as ordinary text from an allowed app may still be recorded.

WPaste has no advertising or analytics SDKs. Diagnostic logs stay on your Mac. If you export logs, WPaste writes a redacted copy to the location you choose. You decide whether to share that file.

WPaste 隐私政策

Mac App Store 版 WPaste 仅在你的 Mac 上保存剪贴板历史，供你查找和再次复制。历史可能包含文本、链接、图片、文件路径、来源 App 和复制时间。文件条目保存文件引用。请将剪贴板历史视为敏感信息。

你可以从菜单栏暂停记录，在设置中排除特定 App 或内容、调整保留期限，也可以删除单条记录或清空历史。WPaste 会忽略带有隐藏标记的内容。允许记录的 App 如果把密码作为普通文本复制，密码仍可能进入历史。

WPaste 不含广告或统计 SDK。诊断日志保存在本机。导出时，WPaste 会把脱敏副本写入你指定的位置，是否分享由你决定。
