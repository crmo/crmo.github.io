WPaste Support

简体中文

WPaste keeps a local history of what you copy on your Mac. Open it from the menu bar or press Shift-Command-V. Select a clip and press Return to copy it, then press Command-V in the destination app.

If history stays empty

Open WPaste from the menu bar and check that recording is on. Copy a new line of text in TextEdit. Check your ignored apps and content rules if only some items are missing. On systems with clipboard privacy controls, check that WPaste can read the clipboard.

Contact

Email crmoalpha@gmail.com with your macOS version, WPaste version and steps to reproduce the issue. Please leave private clipboard contents out of your message.

WPaste 技术支持

WPaste 在本机保存复制历史。点菜单栏图标或按 Shift-Command-V 打开面板，选中一条按回车复制，再回到目标 App 按 Command-V 粘贴。

面板没有记录

在菜单栏确认记录已开启，再到「文本编辑」复制一行新文字。如果只有部分内容缺失，请检查忽略 App 和内容规则。如果系统提供剪贴板隐私设置，请确认 WPaste 拥有读取权限。

请把 macOS 版本、WPaste 版本和复现步骤发送至 crmoalpha@gmail.com。请勿附上私密剪贴板内容。
